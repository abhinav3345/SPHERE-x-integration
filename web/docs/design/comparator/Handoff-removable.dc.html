Removable parts

The twelve things being built tonight. Each is one whole child of a column or one cell of a row, so leaving it out is a matter of not rendering it. Nothing else has to be restyled.

How a gap closes

  • A group that goes from a rail lets the groups under it move up. Groups keep their order and their 12px gaps.
  • At 1280 the timeline is the one part that stretches. It takes whatever height a removed part gives back.
  • A cell that goes from a row lets the others share the row equally.
  • Three rows keep their height when they are empty, so nothing jumps later: the ring row (44px), the legend line (18px) and the chip row of the frame label (24px).
  • A link that names a part that is not there opens Blink with the rest of the link honoured.

With all twelve gone

The screen is what works today, in the same layout.

  • Time rail: search by decimal degrees, the transport and speed, Frames, the caveat list.
  • Image column: the square, the scale bar, readout and compass, the ring row with its hint, the frame label, the legend.
  • View rail: the name and the chip, Band with eight options, Field of view, the note and the Markers toggle, provenance.

Recentre is not one of the twelve. It builds a position in decimal degrees, which works today, so the ring row is never empty once the ring has moved.

Never removed with a feature

  • The frame label, with its reserved chip row.
  • The scale bar and the compass.
  • The hatch, and "No data" in the legend whenever a frame in the sequence has hatched pixels.
  • The caveat list and the greyed, labelled partial and thin rows.
  • The note "Same brightness scale for every frame".
  • The chip "Recorded field" or "Live data".
  • The provenance line and the two DOIs.
