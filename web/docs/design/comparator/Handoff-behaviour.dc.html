Sticky, breakpoints and the keyboard map

How the screen behaves between the sizes that are drawn, what holds its place while the page scrolls, and what every key does. The last two panels list where this design departs from the system's cards and what the working boards only pretend to do.

Sticky and scrolling at 1280 and 1440

  • Nothing is sticky, because the page does not scroll.
  • The timeline is the only part of the time rail that scrolls. It gives up height before anything else and keeps the frame on screen in view.
  • With no frame on screen, the target list takes the timeline's place and scrolls the same way.
  • The spectrum table scrolls inside the panel.
  • The view rail scrolls inside itself if longer text overflows it. It is laid out to fit at the drawn sizes.

Sticky and scrolling on a phone

  • One thing is sticky: the flip dock, at the foot of the screen, for as long as any of the image section is on screen and the key's own slot is below the fold.
  • The app bar and the title strip scroll away with the page.
  • "Change" jumps to "Go to another position" at the foot. "See the targets" jumps to the target list.
  • A notice with a button sits above the image. Every other notice sits under the playback, so the image is never pushed down after it has been seen.
  • Focus never moves to a notice when it appears.

Tab order

At 1280 and 1440. Search, the banner's button, the flip key, Previous, Play, Next, Speed, the timeline rows, the image, Spotlight, Spectrum, Recentre, Copy link, View, Band, Field of view, Contrast, Invert, Markers, the two DOIs.

On a phone. Page order: Copy link, Change, the banner's button, the image, the ring buttons, the flip key, the transport, Speed, the timeline rows, View, Band, Field of view, Contrast, Invert, Markers, Details, the search field, the targets.

The image is one Tab stop. Its focus ring is drawn just inside the edge of the square.

Pointer and touch

  • A click or a tap on the image moves the ring. Nothing else: no drag, no double click, no long press.
  • The flip key flips on pointer down and returns on pointer up, leave or cancel, and when it loses focus. Its long-press menu is suppressed.
  • With a pointer over the image, the readout shows the pixel under it. Otherwise it shows the pixel cursor, and failing that the ring. On a phone that is always the ring.
  • Every target is at least 44px in both directions.
  • Tooltips appear on hover and on focus only: Copy link at 1280, and Recentre. Neither carries a caveat.

Reduced motion and announcements

  • Under reduced motion Play, Speed and the shortcut list are not rendered. Previous, Next and the flip key stay, with the system's note.
  • Nothing plays until Play is pressed: not on load, not after a search, not after a build.
  • The frame label is not a live region. A frame change is announced on step and flip, never during play.
  • The build block is a polite status, updated at most once a second.
  • A search error is an alert. Every other notice is a status.

What a copied link restores

The system asks for five things: position, band, field of view, view and frame.

This design adds four, so the screen comes back as it was left: the flip partner, the ring when it is off centre, whether the spotlight is on, and whether the spectrum is open.

Parameter names are the code's to choose. A link that names a view or a band that is not built opens Blink, or the field's default band, with the rest honoured.

What the working boards only pretend to do

  • Every image is a flat block. Flip, step and play change the block's file name and the label, not pixels.
  • Search reads decimal degrees and the example names. WISE 0855 and Barnard's Star open at once; WISE 0855-0714 opens at its catalogue position; any other valid position opens the building board and stays there. Hours and degrees are read for the system's example only.
  • Field of view moves the lit cell and nothing else. Contrast moves its slider and nothing else. Invert does turn the block from dark to light.
  • Recentre puts the ring back at the centre. It does not start a build.
  • The readout shows the ring or the pixel cursor. It does not follow the pointer.
  • Building does not advance on its own, and the busy count does not tick: the board's State properties set the stage.
  • Arrow keys inside View, Band and Field of view are not wired; clicks are.
  • Copy link shows "Link copied" and copies nothing.

The state switch is in the properties of the boards "Blink, 1280 × 720" and "Blink, 390 × 844": field, cond and stage under State; view, spectrum and spotlight under View.
